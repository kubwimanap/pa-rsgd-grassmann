## Hopkins 155

Download the Hopkins 155 archive from its authors and copy the ground-truth files of the 14 sequences

1R2RC, 1R2RCR, 1R2TCR, 2R3RTC, 2T3RCR (checkerboard), cars1, cars2, cars5, three-cars, truck1 (traffic),
articulated, people1, two_cranes, kanatani1 (articulated and other)

so that `data/hopkins/<sequence>/<sequence>_truth.mat` exists for each (for example
`data/hopkins/1R2RC/1R2RC_truth.mat`). Missing sequences are skipped. Each sequence gives one PCA problem per motion: the centred 2F x P trajectory matrix of that motion,
scaled so that its leading eigenvalue is 4, and its dominant p = 2 dimensional subspace (gap 0.9-2.5).
If the files are absent, the Hopkins columns of Table S4 and the Hopkins row of
Section 3.3 are reported as "not run".
